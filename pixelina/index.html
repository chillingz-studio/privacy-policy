Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Pixelina ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free mobile nonogram puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app.

Information We Collect

The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, or contacts. All game data (such as your puzzle progress, solved levels, remaining hints, and tutorial progress) is stored locally on your device and is never sent to any server. The only information that leaves your device is the advertising and analytics data described below, and any feedback you choose to send.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and optional rewarded ads (for example, watching an ad to earn an extra heart or hint). AdMob may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device identifiers (e.g. advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location (country/region level)
  • App usage and ad interaction data
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size)

This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to personally identifiable information collected by AdMob.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown.

Analytics

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used. Firebase Analytics automatically collects:

  • App usage data (sessions, screen views, engagement time)
  • Device information (model, OS version, screen size, language)
  • General location (country/region level, derived from IP address)
  • App instance identifiers (anonymous installation ID)

We also log custom game events (such as levels started and completed, time spent on a level, mistakes, hints used, and tutorial progress) to improve the game experience. These events do not contain personally identifiable information.

Analytics data is processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We access this data only in aggregate form and cannot identify individual users.

Feedback

If you rate the game 1 to 4 stars, you can type optional feedback. When you tap Submit, that text is sent with Firebase Analytics, together with the level you were on, so we can improve the game. Please don't include personal information in it. Sending feedback is entirely optional.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google AdMob — Ad serving and monetization
    https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics — Usage analytics
    https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Data Security

Game data stored on your device is not transmitted. Data collected by AdMob and Firebase is processed by Google and subject to Google's security measures.

Your Choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads through your device settings:

  • Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID / Delete advertising ID

Analytics data collection by Firebase can be limited through your device's app permissions and Google account activity settings. Uninstalling the app removes all game data stored on your device.

AI-Assisted Development

AI tools were used to assist in the development of this app. All output was reviewed by human developers.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the app with an updated "Last updated" date.
